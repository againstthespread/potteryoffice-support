First steps

Getting started

PotteryOffice has four main tabs: Studio, Inventory, Sell, and Orders. Open Settings from the gear in Studio.

  1. 01
    Set up a workflow

    In Settings, open Workflow templates and name the production steps you use.

  2. 02
    Add a piece or batch

    Start work from Studio. Choose a workflow, then add details as you go. Sample records are optional and can be cleared.

  3. 03
    Record the next real step

    Update progress, add a studio note, or attach photos. Each piece keeps its own stage and history.

02

From clay to kiln

Studio, production & batches

A piece can hold a name, notes, category, location, dimensions, weight, asking price, making cost, clay or glaze references, firing notes, and photographs. Production progress is recorded separately from whether an item is available, reserved, sold, gifted, or archived.

Workflows are templates

When you create a piece, it receives a copy of the selected workflow. Changing a template affects pieces created later; existing pieces keep their own steps and history. You can record a step, repeat an earlier step, skip ahead, or add a correction.

Create a batch when several pieces belong together. Each piece remains an individual record. You can advance eligible pieces to their own next step, mark selected pieces failed, share materials or notes, and split selected members into a new batch.

03

Finished work

Finished Inventory

Inventory keeps production stage and availability as separate details. Use the Active, Sold, Archived, or All scopes, then filter by category, location, stage, batch, clay, glaze, quality, purpose, or other available choices.

For ordinary sale availability, a piece must be finished, available, intended for sale, not failed, and not linked to a commission. Reserved, personal, and commission pieces are excluded from the regular sellable list. The Sell screen has an explicit restricted-stock override for supported cases.

Pieces with sale, refund, or paid-commission history may be protected from deletion so financial records remain connected. Archiving keeps a piece in your records without showing it as active stock.

04

Markets & transactions

Sales Log

Use the Sell tab to record an individual sale or start a named market session. Select pieces, review the checkout, and confirm to record the transaction and mark eligible pieces sold. Past sales and event sales are available from the transaction history.

Sales can include fixed discounts, a remembered tax rate, fees, a payment-method note, and other transaction details. You can record a refund for complete sale lines and choose whether returned pottery goes back to inventory.

Recording only: Cash, Card, Venmo, and Other are labels in a record. PotteryOffice does not charge cards, connect to Venmo, collect money, or send refunds.
05

Customer requests

Commissions & orders

Create an order with a customer name and optional contact text, request details, quantity, agreed price, due date, pickup or shipping notes, and reference photos. Link existing pottery or create pieces for the order. Each linked piece keeps its own production progress.

Record deposits, later payments, and refunds in the order's payment history. Marking an order fulfilled does not create a sale or record a payment. Commission receipts appear separately from inventory sales.

06

Keep a copy you control

Exporting & restoring backups

  1. 01
    Export

    Open Settings, choose Export full backup, then save or share the file to a place you control, such as Files.

  2. 02
    Move the file

    To change devices, transfer the exported backup yourself. PotteryOffice does not automatically sync records between devices.

  3. 03
    Restore

    On the device with PotteryOffice installed, choose Restore a backup in Settings and select the backup file. Restore replaces the active studio after validation; the current studio is preserved locally first.

Backups include records and attached photos. The app does not password-protect or encrypt exported backups. Keep the file somewhere appropriate for any customer details and photographs it contains.

07

A note about your records

Local-only storage

  • The app works offline, and your studio records and photos are stored locally on your device.
  • Automatic cloud or cross-device synchronization is not provided.
  • Removing the app may remove locally stored records. Export a backup before uninstalling or moving to another device.
  • iOS device backups may include app data depending on your backup settings; those backups are managed by Apple or the backup method you choose.

Quick answers

FAQ & troubleshooting

Where are my records stored?

In PotteryOffice's local app storage on this device. There is no automatic sync or developer account. Export a backup before changing devices or uninstalling.

Does a sale charge a customer?

No. The Sell tab records transaction details. It does not process a payment, verify that money changed hands, or send a refund.

Why is a finished piece missing from the usual sales list?

Check that it is marked finished and available, intended for sale, not failed, and not assigned to a commission. Reserved and personal pieces are not ordinary sale stock.

Why didn't a workflow edit change an existing piece?

Pieces keep a copy of their workflow. A template edit applies to newly created pieces; it does not rewrite existing steps or history.

My photo is in iCloud Photos but won't attach.

The iOS photo picker must make a selected image available to the app. Try opening the photo in Photos first so it can download, then select it again.

Can I share an attached photograph?

Open the photograph and choose Share photograph. iOS shows available apps and destinations; your selection determines where a copy is sent.

What happens when I restore?

A validated backup replaces the current studio. Export the current studio first if you may need it; PotteryOffice preserves a local copy during restore as well.

How do I get all my data off this device?

Use Export full backup in Settings and save the file somewhere you control. The backup contains the studio records and attached photos.
