Privacy Policy

PotteryOffice keeps the studio records you create on your device. This policy explains what the app stores, what it does with photos and backups, and what happens when you contact support.

Effective date: November 16, 2026

At a glance

PotteryOffice is an offline-capable recordkeeping app. In the current version, studio records are saved in the app's local storage. The app does not send those records to the developer or a developer-operated server, and it does not provide automatic cloud or cross-device synchronization.

Information you may store in PotteryOffice

You choose what to enter. This can include:

  • Pottery names, descriptions, production steps, dates, notes, measurements, materials, prices, costs, locations, and inventory status.
  • Photographs of pottery, materials, production, or customer references.
  • Customer names and optional contact details, order requirements, due dates, pickup or shipping notes, and commission history.
  • Sales and market-session details, including items, amounts, discounts, tax, fees, payment-method labels, notes, and refunds.

Payment-method labels are notes for your records. PotteryOffice has no payment-card or bank-account fields and does not process payments.

Collection and sharing by the developer

Based on the current app source, project configuration, privacy manifest, and dependencies reviewed for this release, PotteryOffice does not transmit the studio information listed above to the developer or to third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app has no sign-in account, developer-hosted data service, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or automatic sync service.

Apple's system photo and file pickers are used when you choose a photo, camera capture, or backup file. PotteryOffice receives the selected content so it can attach photos or export and restore a backup locally. Use of Apple's operating-system features and any device backup is governed in part by your device settings and Apple's own terms and privacy information.

This description applies to the app version reviewed for the effective date above. If the app's code, included software, or services change, its App Store privacy disclosures and this policy should be reviewed and updated.

Local storage and internet access

PotteryOffice stores its studio database and attached photo files in the app's local storage on your device. Core recordkeeping works without an internet connection. There is no automatic transfer of the database between your devices.

The app does not add a separate password or encryption layer to its exported backup files. This policy does not make a claim about device-level protections provided by iOS or the backup location you choose.

Photos and camera

You can select images with the iOS photo picker or take a photo with the camera when the device has one. The app only imports the images you select or capture. It makes JPEG copies sized to at most 1,800 pixels on the longest edge plus 400-pixel thumbnails; it does not keep the original full-resolution file or upload the app copies to a PotteryOffice server.

A selected photo that is stored in iCloud Photos may need to be downloaded by iOS before it is available to attach. The app does not need a connection to display photos already stored with your records. PotteryOffice does not request broad access to browse your entire photo library or directly save its attached copies back to Photos. You can open the iOS share sheet for an attached image; if you choose another app or destination, that destination receives the image under its own practices.

Exports, restores, and operating-system backups

You can manually export a full backup through the iOS file-sharing interface. It contains your studio records and attached photos. You choose where to save or share it; a location such as iCloud Drive or another file provider may store a copy under that provider's terms. The exported file is not password-protected or encrypted by PotteryOffice, so choose a storage location appropriate for its contents.

Restoring a backup is an explicit action that replaces the active studio after the file is validated. The app preserves a local copy of the previous studio during restore. A restore does not merge two studios.

iOS or a computer backup may also include app data according to the backup settings and method you use. Such operating-system backups are managed by Apple or the provider you choose; PotteryOffice does not receive or control their contents.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain in local app storage until you delete eligible records, replace the studio with a restored backup, or the app's local data is removed. Some pieces with sale, refund, or paid-commission history are protected from deletion so related financial history remains intact. Archiving a record hides it from active work but does not erase it.

There is no PotteryOffice account or developer-held copy to delete. For recovery, the app also keeps a previous-save snapshot and may keep a local safety archive when you restore a backup. Removing an attachment or cancelling a photo draft can leave an unlinked image file in local storage; the app does not currently include a general media-cleanup screen. Removing the app or resetting a device may remove locally stored records and safety copies. Export a backup before uninstalling, replacing a device, or restoring if you need to keep a copy. Backups you exported and device backups are stored separately and must be managed where you saved them.

Contacting support

If you email support, the information in your message and any attachments is sent to the support address through your email provider. The app does not send a support report automatically. Do not include customer information or backup files unless they are needed to resolve your request.

App Store privacy information

For the reviewed app version, the app's privacy manifest declares no collected data and no tracking. The app's local-only studio records are not sent to the developer by the app. App Store Connect disclosures should be checked against the final submitted build and any services or software included in it.

Changes to this policy

If PotteryOffice's data practices change, this policy will be updated and its effective date changed. The current version will be available from Settings in the app and from the support website.
